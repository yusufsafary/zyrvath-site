02 · Claim

Holders draw $ZYR by signal strength.

Every received transmission entitles its holder to a one-time claim of $ZYR, the station token. Claims follow the work, not the wallet that received it, and the amount follows the signal the work carries: stronger messages draw more. Full rules live in the claim handbook at zyrvath.com/zyrvath-claim-skill.md.
