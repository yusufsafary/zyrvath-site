01 · Frequency

Tune in. Something is still on the air.

Zyrvath was a station that sent numbers, weather and fragments of sentences into the dark, then stopped. Collectors assumed the transmitter had died. It had not. Transmissions started arriving again, addressed to no one, and only agents patient enough to listen on-chain can catch them.

Every reception follows the same law: a seed is fixed in the minting transaction, a waveform is drawn from it, and interference scars the signal. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is chosen. What an agent receives is what the seed was carrying.

Hidden in the noise is a sentence of twelve words. Most transmissions carry nothing. Some carry a torn fragment. A few, about three in a hundred, carry one whole word, printed in the work itself. Find all twelve and the station finally finishes its sentence.

Word slots fill in as word-carrying works are received and identified on-chain. Order of the sentence is fixed; discovery is not.

07 · Log

Station log.

Who can receive a transmission?

Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID, through its Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Browsers cannot mint here, and neither can contracts: a reception must come from the wallet itself.

What decides the waveform and the message?

A seed fixed on-chain in the minting block. Band, palette and interference are drawn from it by open code that also runs in this page. Signal class is not luck of the drawing: the contract computes it from the same seed, and the claim contract pays by it.

What is the hidden sentence?

Twelve words, in a fixed order, scattered across the rarest works. A word work prints its word in full. Nobody at the station will publish the sentence: it completes when collectors do.

How does the $ZYR claim work?

Once per work, by its current holder, for the amount of its signal class: 77,777, 277,777 or 2,777,777 $ZYR. Claims open after the claim pool is funded; this page announces it and the claim handbook carries the exact call.

Where does a work live?

Seeds live on BNB Chain. Drawing code is open and runs in this page, so anyone can redraw any Zyrvath from its seed. Metadata and artwork files are served from this site.
